# Sample school demo patterns

`apps/api/scripts/seed-sample-school.ts` seeds a deterministic ~300-student
Kenyan primary school under a school named `TEST — Kilimani Primary School`
(or a custom `--school-name "TEST — ..."`). Every "random" value in the
script is generated by a seeded PRNG (`createSeededRng()` in
`apps/api/scripts/lib/kenyan-names.ts`), so **every seed run produces the
exact same names, admission numbers, dates, and scores** listed below. This
was verified across three full live seed→teardown cycles plus one
documentation-capture run — all four produced identical demo-case data.

School term: **Term 2, 2026**, `2026-05-04` to `2026-07-24`.

## Declining student — grade drop over the term

**Kanini Owino**, Grade 6B, admission **`GRADE6B003`**.

Mathematics and Science & Technology CAT/exam scores start strong (75-85%)
and fall sharply from CAT 3 onward (35-46%) — every other subject is
unaffected, so the drop is visible specifically when filtering to
Math/Science on this student's gradebook:

| Assessment | Date | Math | Science & Tech |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAT 1 | 2026-05-14 | 75.58 | 79.53 |
| CAT 2 | 2026-05-26 | 84.30 | 80.34 |
| Mid-term Exam | 2026-06-05 | 78.33 | 75.36 |
| CAT 3 | 2026-06-17 | 42.93 | 42.06 |
| CAT 4 | 2026-06-29 | 35.45 | 41.61 |
| End-term Exam | 2026-07-09 | 41.30 | 45.60 |

Attendance also degrades in the second half of term (95% present / rare late
in the first half, dropping to ~45% present with frequent absences after).

Two `behaviour_points` NEGATIVE entries, both in the second half of term:
- 2026-06-22 — "Unkind to a classmate" (4 points)
- 2026-07-06 — "Disruptive during lesson" (4 points)

## Linked homework — Grade 5A Mathematics

The homework titled **"CAT 1 Homework"** (due **2026-05-11**) for Grade 5A
Mathematics is linked to the gradebook: every graded completion cascades
into a `grades` row on assessment **"Mathematics Homework (linked)"**
(`max_marks: 100`, `source_type: 'HOMEWORK'`) via the `write_linked_grade()`
RPC — never a direct `grades` insert, which the
`grades_block_direct_edit_on_linked` trigger would reject.

## Linked quiz — Grade 7A Science and Technology

**"Science and Technology Quiz 1"** for Grade 7A is linked to the gradebook
the same way: attempts cascade into assessment **"Science and Technology
Quiz (linked)"** (`max_marks: 100`, `source_type: 'QUIZ'`) via
`write_linked_grade()`, replicating `record_quiz_grade()`'s own
`ROUND((score/max_score) * 100, 2)` normalization inline (that RPC itself is
never called from the seed script — see the script's own comments for why).

## Unmatched Paybill payment
