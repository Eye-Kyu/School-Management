## What SKULI does for your school today

Your school's day-to-day runs through one system: attendance, timetables, fee balances, homework, gradebooks, report cards, quizzes, parent messaging, and a document library — all in one place, for admins, teachers, students, and parents.

Since your school came on board, we've spent the last few weeks making three specific things better: getting fee messages to parents faster, making it easier to pay fees the way most parents already do, and preparing your student records for government reporting.

## What's new

**Parents get an SMS the moment something needs their attention.** When a student is marked absent, their parent now gets a text message, not just an in-app notice — no more finding out at pickup time. This works alongside the email notifications you already had.

**Parents can now pay school fees the way they already do — straight from their M-Pesa, via your school's own Paybill number.** No app download, no new steps for the parent: they pay like they always have, and the balance on their child's account updates itself within a few minutes, with no admin action needed. Every payment — however it came in — gets a proper PDF receipt automatically.

**Admins can see and match payments that didn't auto-match.** Sometimes a parent's payment reference doesn't line up perfectly with a student record. There's now a dedicated screen for an admin to look at those and match them by hand in a couple of clicks, instead of digging through M-Pesa statements.

**Your student records are ready for NEMIS/KEMIS submission.** Admins can now export a properly formatted file of your school's student data — the fields the Ministry asks for — directly from the system, ready to use for government reporting.

**Behind the scenes:** we ran a security and accessibility review across the most-used screens in the system — login, dashboards, gradebook, attendance, fees — and fixed everything that review found, including a subtle bug where a scheduled background job could, in rare cases, send the same SMS twice.

## What's coming next

Two things we planned for this stretch are still waiting on outside parties, not on us:

- **WhatsApp notifications** — waiting on approval from Meta (WhatsApp's parent company), which can take a few weeks once submitted.
- **CBC competency tracking and report cards** — waiting on confirmation of the exact competency list and a sample report card from the Ministry, so what we build matches what KICD actually expects.

Both are next in line the moment those approvals come through — we haven't dropped them, they're paced by someone else's clock, not ours.

## Why this matters for you

1. **Fewer missed messages.** SMS reaches a parent who doesn't check email or the app — the channel most Kenyan parents actually use.
2. **Fee collection with less friction, for you and for parents.** Paybill means parents pay the way they already know how, and your books update themselves instead of needing a staff member to reconcile M-Pesa statements by hand.
3. **One less thing to worry about at reporting time.** NEMIS/KEMIS export means your student data is a few clicks away from ready, not a manual re-typing exercise.
